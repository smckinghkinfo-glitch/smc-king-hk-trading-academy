FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Smart Money Concepts (SMC)?

SMC is a way of reading price through liquidity, market structure, order blocks and imbalances. In this program you learn it only after candlesticks, support and resistance, chart patterns, indicators and price action.

Do I need prior trading experience?

No. Month 1 starts with how the markets work, order types and charting, then builds up step by step.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Live. Classes run Monday to Friday, with a live market session on Saturday and a mentorship call on Sunday.

What if I do not understand a topic within the 3 months?

You can revise it by joining any running batch. Our responsibility is to train you properly, and the practice and hard work have to come from you.

What is the daily psychology class?

A 30-minute class every day, from Day 1, alongside the learning. It covers the mindset side of trading: fear, greed, FOMO, revenge trading, patience, discipline, handling losses and journaling, and it is part of the course in all 3 months.

What markets can I trade after the course?

The program covers the Indian market (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, stocks, F&O), Indian commodities (MCX), crypto, global commodities such as Gold and Crude Oil, and global currency pairs.

Do you offer a refund?

Yes. A full course fee refund is available within the first 7 days under the academy's written refund process. Terms are shared at enrollment.
